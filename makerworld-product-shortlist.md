## ⚠️ Popular right now but NOT free to sell

- **Meccha Chameleon figures** (viral this month, 20k+ downloads in days) — MakerWorld Exclusive License, commercial use **prohibited**.
- **Wiggly Kitty flexi** (BumLab3D, 29k downloads) — selling requires joining the designer's paid membership. Cheap option if cat flexis sell well for you.
- Rule of thumb: check the **License** box at the bottom of every model page. Only sell **Public Domain / CC-BY / CC-BY-SA**, or licenses where you've bought/joined membership. Use the filter `License → BY` when browsing.
- Branded characters (Stitch, Pokémon, Mario, Toothless itself) carry IP risk regardless of model license — enforcement in Georgia is unlikely but Facebook can take down listings on IP reports.

## Facebook page activity plan

1. **Post the movement, not the object.** Flexi toys sell on video — 10-second clips of the dragon wiggling in your hand outperform photos. Film in daylight, vertical, post as Reels (Reels get the most reach in GE).
2. **3 posts/week rhythm:** one product video, one "printer time-lapse" (people love watching prints), one interactive post (poll: "რომელი ფერი დავბეჭდო შემდეგი?" / "which color next?").
3. **Color-vote posts** — let followers pick next week's colorway. Free engagement + tells you what to stock.
4. **Bundle offers:** dragon + baby dragon, shark + octopus "ocean set," 3 fidgets for the price of 2. Bundles raise average order without new models.
5. **Seasonal hooks:** school break fidgets, New Year ornaments/gifts (Dec is huge), Mariamoba/birthday gift angle.
6. **Price anchors in GEL in every post** — posts without a price get "ფასი?" comments and lower conversion.
7. Cross-post to Facebook Marketplace and local groups (e.g., Tbilisi buy/sell groups) — that's where Georgian toy buyers actually search.
8. When selling CC-BY models, put "Design by <name>, CC-BY" in the post text — it's the license requirement and costs nothing.
